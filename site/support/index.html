Common questions

I lost my progress

A guest burrow lives only on your device. If you signed in with Google or with your email, sign in again with the same account on your new device and your burrow comes back. A guest burrow that was never saved cannot be recovered.

How do I delete my account?

From the Game, in a few taps, or by email: see Delete your account.
