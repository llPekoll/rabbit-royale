How it plays

Dig, grow your burrow, raid the neighbours.

Read the numbers

Every tile you open tells you how many bombs are next to it. Work out where it's safe, dig your way to the chests, and grab them before the other rabbits on the island do.

Up to 4 rabbits per island, live

Guess wrong, fly home

Step on a bomb and you're blasted back to where you came from. Nobody dies, but everyone saw it. Back home, fence your burrow and plant bombs of your own.

Your burrow is a minefield you design

Pay the neighbours a visit

From level 3, sail to other players' burrows. Zap their rabbit, push it into the sea, and leave with the carrots they didn't guard.
