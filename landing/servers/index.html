One account, everywhere

Until now, Europe, Asia and America were three separate worlds: one account per region, and a trip took you back to an old burrow. Now there is only one account, one burrow and one leaderboard for everyone.

What about ping?

Islands are still played on the server closest to you. Right before each island, the game measures the ping and picks the server. If the choice looks wrong, you can force a server in Settings → Server (Auto by default).

  • Islands: with the players on your server, so nobody lags.
  • Raids: any burrow in the world, and yours can be raided from anywhere. Live defence works from one continent to another.
  • Leaderboard: one, worldwide.

I played in several regions: what do I keep?

  • Your most advanced burrow: the one whose rabbit has the highest level, then the highest burrow, then the most carrots ever earned. On a tie, the one you played last.
  • Everything you bought, in any region: skins, Crown Race tickets, purchases.
  • Your history of games and raids.
  • Your earliest arrival date and your latest connection.

What stayed in the other copies (their burrow, bombs, planks and energy) does not come back.

If you had a guest account linked to your wallet in one region and a wallet account in another, they become one account.

Every region was backed up right before the merge. If you think you lost something, tell us on Discord.

One device at a time

Your account plays on one device at a time. If you open the game somewhere else, the first device shows Other device, and Play here takes it back. The island you were on is banked, nothing is lost. A simple network drop does not count: the game reconnects and you get your island back.
